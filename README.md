## 📊 Projects Included

### 1. Sales Analysis & Customer Segmentation Dashboard
**Preview:**
![Sales Analysis Dashboard](image_1daa56.jpg)

* **Objective**: A deep-dive operational analysis of sales performance, product profitability, and customer demographics.
* **Key Metrics (from image_1daa56.jpg)**: 
    * **Total Sales**: $2,297,201
    * **Total Quantity**: 37,873 units
    * **Total Orders**: 9,994
    * **Avg. Discount**: 16%
    * **Total Profit**: $286,397
* **Key Visuals**: 
    * **Category Breakdown**: Detailed doughnut charts for Profit, Quantity, and Sales by category (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies).
    * **Product Performance**: "Top 10 Products" bar chart and analysis of sales/profit by sub-category.
    * **Geographic Insights**: Map visualization showing "Total Customer by State".
    * **Interactivity**: Fully dynamic filtering by Years (2015-2018), Quarters, and Regions.
